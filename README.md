**😄 Connecting technology governance, software engineering, and responsible AI.**

I bring over 25 years of experience in software engineering, IT governance, technology management, and digital transformation in Brazil's public sector. 

🔭 I previously worked at [Brazil's Superior Electoral Court (TSE)](https://international.tse.jus.br/en) and currently work at the [Brazilian Chamber of Deputies](https://www2.camara.leg.br/english), where my activities include IT governance, strategic technology planning, IT procurement and contract management, and advising on software engineering initiatives.

## 🌱 Areas of focus

- **IT Governance & Strategy** — Aligning technology decisions, planning, procurement, and contracts with organizational priorities.
- **Software Engineering & Architecture** — Advising on software initiatives, with a focus on architecture, maintainability, and secure development.
- **Artificial Intelligence, Privacy & Cybersecurity** — Exploring how AI can support secure software development, as well as exploring security and responsible AI.

🤔 I’m currently learning about:
  - Privacy Engineering / Secure Software Development;
  - AI in software development;
  - Platform Engineering;

## 🎓 Education & research

I completed a **Professional Master's Degree in <a href="https://ppee.unb.br/" target="_blank">Electrical Engineering</a> at the <a href="https://international.unb.br/" target="_blank">University of Brasília (UnB)</a> in 2026**. My dissertation, *“Evaluating Architectural Strategies for Personal Data Detection in Java Source Code Using Transformer Classifiers and Large Language Models,”* examined approaches to detecting personal data in Java source code.

My academic background also includes a Bachelor of Science (BS) in Computer Science, a specialist degree in Software Engineering, and an MBA in Leadership and Development of High Performance Teams. I earned the [AWS Certified Machine Learning – Specialty](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-machine-learning-specialty/) certification in **2024**.
